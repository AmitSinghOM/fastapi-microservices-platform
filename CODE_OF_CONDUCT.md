# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a harassment-free, welcoming community for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, empathetic, and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect differing viewpoints and boundaries.
- Focus on what is best for users and the community.
- Protect private information and security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, trolling, insults,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/AmitSinghOM/fastapi-microservices-platform/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct report`. Maintainers will limit details to
people needed to investigate, avoid conflicts of interest, and may warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants. Knowingly false or
retaliatory reports may also result in action.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Enforcement decisions should explain the violated rule while
protecting reporter privacy.

This policy is informed by Contributor Covenant 2.1 and Mozilla's Community
Participation Guidelines.
